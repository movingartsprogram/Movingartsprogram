ABOUT LIVE MUSIC WITH MAP

Moving Arts Program is a pro-bono live music platform in Goa, run from Benaulim, South Goa. Every rupee from tickets goes to the artists and the volunteers. Alongside one-off nights, MAP runs six series:

  • Grateful Dead Tribute Festival
  • The Classics
  • Unaccompanied: solo, original sets
  • Rock Revival
  • The Outcasts
  • Vinyl Resurrection with Bobby

Want MAP to play your event? Tap "Customised Private Gigs" on the home page. Meet Elvis Lobo, The Boss, or follow the MAP Community channel for every new gig.
