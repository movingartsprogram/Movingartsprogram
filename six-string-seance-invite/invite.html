Six-String Séance

Tarot card readers, magicians, face painters, mentalists, Bobby on vinyl, and the one and only Elvis Lobo on six strings.

The plan: a baithak, not a gig. No stage, no rows, just a circle. Count the chairs before you sit.

Bobby spins as you arrive. Faces get painted, cards get read. Magicians and mentalists work the circle. Elvis takes over. Then Bobby and Elvis jam together. Stories follow, dirty or laundry-clean.

Avant-garde B-grade pulp horror, cheap fangs and all.

Adults only.

Thursday 29 October, Halloween eve
