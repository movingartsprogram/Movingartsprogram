QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

When and where is it?

Sunday 22 November 2026. Gates open at 7 PM, music from 8 PM.
Domingos Gazebo, Pedda Road, Varca, South Goa 403721. Open in Google Maps

Who is playing?

Bombay Rock Xchange, on their first Goa tour: Luke Kenny (vocals), Ravi Iyer (guitar), Saket Rao (drums) and Tejal (bass). Classic rock and Bollywood, live, with a surprise artist from Goa.

Is there another Bombay Rock Xchange show?

Yes. Saturday 21 November at Saltamontes, Anjuna, featuring Ravi Iyer and Luke Kenny. Tickets for that night are at the gate only. Online booking is for 22 November at Domingos Gazebo, Varca.

How much are tickets?

Early Bird ₹499: first 50 tickets, until 22 October.
General ₹799: 23 October to 21 November.
At the gate ₹1000: on the night, 22 November.

How do I book?

Online at movingartsprogram.com/gig-bookings. Up to 10 tickets per booking. Pay securely by card, UPI, net banking or wallet.

How do tickets work at the gate?

Every person gets their own QR. Send each ticket to the person who will use it. At the gate each QR is scanned once and you get a wristband, which is your pass for the night, so keep it on. A QR that has already been scanned will not work again.

I lost my ticket. What do I do?

Open the booking page and use FIND MY TICKETS. They are also in the WhatsApp chat or email you sent them to after booking.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking at the venue.

Is there food and a bar?

Yes, food and drinks are available to buy at the venue. They are not included in the ticket.

Can I bring children?

Yes, it is all ages. Children must be with an adult, and everyone who comes in needs their own ticket.

Can I get a refund?

If MAP cancels, you get every rupee back within 7 working days, automatically. If you can't make it, we can't refund you. Full refund terms.

Where does my money go?

MAP is a pro-bono live music platform. Every rupee goes to the artists and the volunteers. MAP keeps nothing.
